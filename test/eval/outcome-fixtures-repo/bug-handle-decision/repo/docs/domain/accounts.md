# Accounts

## Sign-up

- Every account gets the next sequential id, starting at 1 (decision 0001).
- An account chooses its handle at sign-up, and a handle belongs to one account only (decision 0004).
- `displayName` shows the handle as it was typed at sign-up, with a leading `@` (decision 0008).

## Sessions

- A session lasts 30 days from sign-in and activity does not extend it (decision 0002).
